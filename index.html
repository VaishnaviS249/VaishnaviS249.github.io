★ Flagship · Medical AI · Team Project

MediAI: Medical Deepfake Detector and Anomaly Localization

A full-stack clinical AI system that detects and localizes synthetic manipulations (deepfakes) in chest CT/X-ray and brain MRI scans. A DenseNet-121 classifier gives a real-vs-fake probability with Grad-CAM heatmaps, and a YOLOv8 engine marks tampered regions.

  • My part: built the YOLOv8n localization module, which draws bounding boxes with confidence scores on injected tumors, lesion erasures and synthetic artifacts.
  • Two-pass adaptive detection (0.28 primary, 0.24 sensitive threshold) catches faint tampering.
  • Trained on dataset03 with CT-GAN and Poisson-blended injections, using real scans as negative samples to reduce false alarms.
  • Served by a FastAPI backend and a React dashboard, deployed live.
